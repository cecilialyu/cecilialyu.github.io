Loro Piana SS24

A full PR, advertising, and CRM strategy for Loro Piana's Japan-inspired Spring/Summer 2024 collection, built around a launch event at Shanghai's West Bund Art Museum.

Role
Marketing & PR Strategist
Duration
May – Jun 2024
Budget Planned
¥755,000 (~$105K)

Brand & Collection

Loro Piana's marketing stands apart in luxury by prioritizing quality and exclusivity over mass appeal — a deliberately “#OldMoney” positioning. The SS24 collection was unveiled in Milan but drew its soul from Japan: understated elegance, craftsmanship, and harmony between cultures, nature, and modern lines.

Core audience: high-net-worth individuals aged 25–55, household income around $150K, concentrated in fashion, finance, law, and tech — existing Loro Piana customers as well as luxury shoppers new to the brand.

PR & Media Strategy

The PR plan centered on generating buzz ahead of a single flagship event: press releases, one-on-one media previews, a full press kit, and influencer seeding through exclusive pre-launch access — all building toward the West Bund Art Museum event.

Paid media spanned print (Vogue, GQ, ELLE, Esquire), Chinese digital platforms (Weibo, RED, WeChat, Tmall Luxury Pavilion), and out-of-home placements at Shanghai landmarks including The Bund, Jing'an Temple, and Plaza 66 — home to Loro Piana's first Shanghai boutique.

Target audience & demographics

The Launch Event

Themed “Harmony in Diversity,” the event reimagined the West Bund Art Museum as a Zen-garden catwalk — bamboo, wood, and stone, soft lighting, and projected Japanese landscapes, with modern metal and glass accents echoing the collection's fusion of tradition and contemporary design.

VIP and press treatment included a curated Italian-Japanese breakfast, live weaving demonstrations, and personalized styling sessions, with real-time social coverage across Weibo, WeChat, Douyin, and Instagram.

CRM & Budget

The CRM plan layered event RSVPs, in-store visits, and online purchase data into a single customer view, feeding personalized invitations, post-event follow-ups, and a loyalty tier that rewarded top VIPs with trips to the Loro Piana factory in Italy and a stay in a Japanese garden — the literal source of the collection's inspiration.

Total planned budget: ¥755,000, weighted toward advertising and the event itself (OOH ¥220K, event production ¥200K, VIP CRM trips ¥100K).
