Brighton Farmer's Market

A full brand identity, website, and app concept for a local produce market, built around the campaign line “Let's Harvest Together.”

The Market

Agriculture in the Brighton area runs small to medium-sized produce vending, constrained by an urban footprint that makes large-scale growing or livestock difficult. The opportunity was to sell freshness and community, not scale: superior quality, a direct-from-farm supply chain, and eco-friendly practices.

Target audience: individuals aged 25–45, upper income, mid-career professionals who cook regularly, value sustainability, and are likely raising a family.

Market produce

Campaign Concept

“Let's Harvest Together” puts the faces and stories behind local farms front and center, pairing farmer profiles with local-influencer partnerships to turn a transactional grocery run into a sense of community ownership over the harvest.

Brand Identity

The mark is a squirrel — clean, user-friendly, minimalist — paired with a warm, produce-inspired palette (forest green, near-black, harvest gold, blush, berry red, and leaf green) and a serif/slab type pairing that reads as both artisanal and legible on shelf tags and signage.

Website & App

The website leads with the campaign line and a full-bleed field photo, then routes into delivery, a seasonal calendar, and vendor pages. The companion app mirrors the same visual system for browsing by category — produce, dairy, drinks, flowers — with a soft loading animation built around the squirrel mark.
